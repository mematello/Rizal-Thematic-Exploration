4. EXPLANATION OF WHY pag-ibig AND kamatayan validation_entered DIFFERS FROM GOLDEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observation:
  - 'pag-ibig':  Golden validation_entered = 160 (skipped = 141, total = 301)
                 HEAD   validation_entered = 137 (skipped = 164, total = 301)
  - 'kamatayan': Golden validation_entered = 172 (skipped = 131, total = 303)
                 HEAD   validation_entered = 123 (skipped = 180, total = 303)
  - 'kalayaan':  Golden validation_entered = 158 (skipped = 146, total = 304)
                 HEAD   validation_entered = 128 (skipped = 176, total = 304)
  - 'edukasyon': Golden validation_entered = 194 (skipped = 106, total = 300)
                 HEAD   validation_entered = 194 (skipped = 106, total = 300)

Detailed Mechanism:
1. Total Candidates Evaluated is Fixed:
   For every query, `validation_entered + validation_skipped` is strictly invariant across commits.
   Candidates either match Check 1 (Theme/valid_tokens check) or enter Check 2 (Dynamic validation).
2. In Pre-E3 / Golden (set iteration over sent_words):
   `sent_words` was iterated in arbitrary hash-seed order. When dynamic validation checked words,
   the first word encountered with `word_sim_cache[w] > 0.55` was matched and appended to `valid_tokens`.
   Because set iteration order varied by process hash seed, different words were added to `valid_tokens`.
3. In Commit E3 / HEAD (`sorted(sent_words)`):
   `sent_words` is sorted alphabetically. In candidate sentences with multiple synonym words,
   the alphabetically earlier word is consistently evaluated and added to `valid_tokens` first.
   Many of these alphabetically earlier words are common Tagalog roots/prefixes that appear across multiple
   subsequent sentences. Consequently, MORE subsequent candidates match immediately at Check 1
   (`if token in text_lower: has_validation = True`), skipping dynamic validation entirely.
4. Pass/Fail Status is Unchanged for Core Queries:
   For 'pag-ibig', 'kamatayan', and 'kalayaan', exactly 0 candidates changed pass/fail status.
   100% of candidate sentences that passed in Golden continue to pass in HEAD.
   The only change is the stage of validation: 23 candidates for 'pag-ibig' and 49 candidates for 'kamatayan'
   matched at Check 1 (fast path) rather than entering Check 2 (dynamic encoding path).
