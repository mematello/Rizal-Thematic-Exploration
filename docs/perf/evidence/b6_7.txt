1. REAL NETWORK STATE DURING proc1..proc6 EXECUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Investigation of Translator Behavior during proc1..proc6:
  - In proc1..proc6, 10 queries triggered calls to `_translator_cache.translate()`:
    ['korapsyon', 'karahasan ng mga prayle', 'tiktok', 'bitcoin', 'covid',
     'facebook', 'internet', 'computer', 'cellphone', 'online']
  - When live GoogleTranslator(source='auto', target='tl').translate() was called,
    the unauthenticated public endpoint returned:
      `deep_translator.exceptions.TooManyRequests: Server Error: You made too many requests to the server.`
  - TranslatorCache.translate() caught this exception inside its ThreadPoolExecutor,
    executed `self.set_failure(text)`, and returned `(None, True)`.
  - Consequently, in all six processes (proc1..proc6), every unmapped query returned `output=None`.
  - Because the negative cache behavior was identical across all processes, zero divergence
    occurred due to network flakiness. However, live Google was blocked by bot-rate-limiting.
