4. COUNTERFACTUAL TABLES AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B3 Claim: Counterfactual ranking comparison between current key and unrounded final desc, with claims of 'improved relevance' based on mean semantic score.
Verification status: RETRACTED / CLARIFIED.
Evidence:
  - Column A in docs/perf/counterfactual_report.json used golden_snapshot_deterministic.json (HEAD).
  - For semantic_fallback queries, the final 10-candidate list is decided by 'rerank' (not unrounded final).
  - Inferring 'improved relevance' from higher mean semantic score is mathematically invalid:
    semantic score measures XLM-RoBERTa cosine similarity, not human ground-truth thematic relevance.
    Higher cosine similarity can favor generic sentences with high lexical overlap rather than nuanced matches.
